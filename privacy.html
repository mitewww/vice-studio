Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Vice Studio is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to edit their own gaming clips and post them to their own social media accounts. It has no other users.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, Vice Studio receives basic profile information (display name and avatar) and permission to post videos the owner chooses to that account.

How it is used

Only to show which account is connected and to post the owner's own videos when the owner tells it to.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any server run by Vice Studio; there is none.

Removal

The owner can disconnect at any time in Vice Studio, or remove access in TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions. Deleting Vice Studio deletes all stored data.
