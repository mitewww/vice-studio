Turn gaming clips into ready‑to‑post vertical videos.

Vice Studio is a desktop video studio for gaming creators. Cut a clip, lay out the gameplay and webcam, add captions and music, then post the finished video to your own TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account.

Windows desktop app9:16, 1:1 and 16:9Runs on your own computer

What it does

Everything a gaming clip needs before it goes out, in one place.

Edit

Trim and cut clips, stack the webcam over the gameplay, zoom in on key moments and add transitions.

Captions

Automatic word-by-word captions, made on your own computer, that you can correct and style.

Text and sound

Titles, channel tags, background music and sound effects, with even loudness for phones.

Any shape

Vertical 9:16 for TikTok and Shorts, square 1:1, or widescreen 16:9 for longer YouTube videos.

Post

Send the finished video to your connected TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account, with your caption and privacy choice.

Schedule

Pick a time and the video goes out then, while Vice Studio is running on your computer.

Posting to TikTok

Vice Studio uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. You stay in control of every post.

1

Connect your account

Sign in with TikTok and approve access. Vice Studio shows which account is connected.

2

Choose the details

Before each post you write the caption and choose who can see it, and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed.

3

Press Post

The video is uploaded to your own TikTok account. Nothing is posted unless you choose it.

PermissionWhat it is used for
user.info.basicShows the name and picture of the connected account, so you know where the video goes.
video.publishPosts the video you finished to your profile, with the caption and privacy you chose.
video.uploadSends a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, to finish in the TikTok app.

Sign-in tokens stay on your own computer. You can disconnect at any time in Vice Studio, or in TikTok under Settings › Security › Manage app permissions. Read the Privacy Policy.
